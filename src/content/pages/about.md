## About the lab

The Cambridge Health Justice Lab studies how the financing, coverage, accessibility and quality of health care affect people who are poor, uninsured or face discrimination. The lab is based in the Department of Medicine at Cambridge Health Alliance and Harvard Medical School.

## History

Drs. Steffie Woolhandler, David Himmelstein and David Bor founded the lab in 1982 at Cambridge Hospital and Harvard Medical School. Dr. Danny McCormick has led it since 2010.

## Research contributions

Lab members have published more than 200 scientific articles, including widely cited studies of:

- medical bankruptcy
- the high administrative costs of the US health care system
- the consequences of being uninsured
- the effects of for-profit ownership of health care providers and insurers
- profit-driven misconduct by drug companies
- immigrants' outsized contributions to paying for US health care
- gaps in mental health care for minority youth
- the role of minority physicians in caring for underserved patients
- racism built into clinical algorithms
- how the Affordable Care Act changed access to care
- how often sexual assault happens to young women, and what it costs them
