The lab works with medical students from Harvard Medical School and residents in the [Cambridge Health Alliance Internal Medicine Residency](https://www.challiance.org/academics/medicine/internal-medicine-residency). Their work with us has appeared in leading medical journals.

Cambridge Health Alliance is a public hospital system affiliated with Harvard Medical School. It is the birthplace of three physician advocacy organizations: Physicians for Social Responsibility, Physicians for Human Rights and Physicians for a National Health Program.

The lab is the training and mentorship site for the [Harvard Medical School Fellowship in General Medicine and Primary Care](https://www.hmsgenmedfellowship.org), which trains physicians in biostatistics, epidemiology and health services research.
