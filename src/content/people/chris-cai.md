Christopher Cai, MD, MS, is an Assistant Professor at the University of Minnesota Medical School and a primary care internist at the Community-University Health Care Center in Minneapolis. His research evaluates health financing reforms, including Medicare payment reform, private equity in medicine and prescription drug policy. He earned his MD from the University of California San Francisco and a master's in health policy and management from the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, and completed his residency in primary care internal medicine and a research fellowship at Brigham and Women's Hospital. He previously worked as a policy fellow in the US House of Representatives, and his work on Medicare for All financing has been published in PLoS Medicine.
