Dhara Patel's research interests include climate change-related health outcomes. She completed her Internal Medicine residency at Penn State Health, where she served as chief resident and worked as a medical fellow with the Hospital and Ambulatory Policy Group at the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services. She received her DO degree from Edward Via College of Osteopathic Medicine – Blacksburg and completed her BA at the University of Virginia.
