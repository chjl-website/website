Emily Lupez, MD, MPH, is a General Medicine and Primary Care Research Fellow at CHA and Harvard Medical School. Her research interests include health inequities in vulnerable populations, social determinants of health, and access to care. She is originally from Durham, North Carolina and completed her BS in Biomedical Engineering at North Carolina State University and her MD at Wake Forest School of Medicine. Prior to medical school she completed her MPH at University of South Florida and recently completed her residency in Internal Medicine at Boston Medical Center.
