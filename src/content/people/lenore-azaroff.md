Lenore Azaroff is a primary care internist at the Edward M. Kennedy Community Health Center in Worcester, Massachusetts. She holds a doctorate in environmental health from the Harvard School of Public Health and spent many years as an occupational health researcher at the University of Massachusetts Lowell and the Massachusetts Department of Public Health, studying underreporting of workplace injuries, the safety of immigrant workers, and access to workers' compensation. She received her MD from Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine and completed a residency at Cambridge Health Alliance. Her research includes studies of child deaths in states with abortion bans, heat- and cold-related illness, and medical journals' coverage of war.
