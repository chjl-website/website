Liz Schrier is an internal medicine resident at University of California, San Francisco. Her research interests include healthcare financialization and health workforce unionization. She previously worked at Dana-Farber Cancer Institute, Partners in Health, and Erie Family Health Center, a community health center in Chicago. She plans to pursue a career in academic internal medicine.
