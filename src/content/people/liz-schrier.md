Liz Schrier is an assistant professor of medicine at University of California, San Francisco in the Division of Hospital Medicine at the San Francisco VA Medical Center. Her research interests include healthcare financialization and health workforce unionization. She previously worked at Dana-Farber Cancer Institute, Partners in Health, and Erie Family Health Center, a community health center in Chicago.
