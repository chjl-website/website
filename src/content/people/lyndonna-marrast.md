Assistant Professor of Medicine at the Donald and Barbara Zucker School of Medicine at Hofstra/Northwell. Trained in internal medicine, her additional roles include researcher, educator, mentor and coach. She teaches interprofessional trainees on topics of health equity and forms community-academic partnerships to increase the health literacy of the general public.
