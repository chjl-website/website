Samuel Dickman, MD, is a health services researcher and the Medical Director at Planned Parenthood of Montana. His research focuses on health disparities, reproductive health, substance use, and incarceration. Dr. Dickman received an MD from Harvard Medical School and completed a residency in internal medicine at San Francisco General Hospital/UC San Francisco.
